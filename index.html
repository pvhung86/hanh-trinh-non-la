🎼 Hà Nội pronunciation guide · tones and sounds
Ngang · no markKeep the voice level and steady.
Huyền · graveLet the voice fall gently.
Sắc · acuteLet the voice rise.
Hỏi · hookDip, then turn upward.
Ngã · tildeRise with a slight voice break in careful Hà Nội speech.
Nặng · dotKeep it short, low and firm.

In careful Hà Nội pronunciation, hỏi and ngã are distinct. In Hà Nội speech, d, gi and often r share a voiced z-like sound, while đ is a different consonant. The speaker button uses a Vietnamese voice available on the device and may not be a Hà Nội speaker. These cues are a memory aid; listen to a trusted family or teacher model for the full sound.

Voice recognition uses your browser’s vi-VN service, which may not reproduce or assess a Hà Nội accent precisely. It checks the words it hears — not whether you sound native. Typed practice is always available.
